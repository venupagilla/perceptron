Averages

The Soul of the Group — Complete Study Notes

1. Foundational Philosophy & Core Definitions

The "Equal Distribution" Mindset

  • An average is the point of perfect equilibrium — the "Soul of the Group."
  • Family Roti Analogy: Family of 5 eats 7, 4, 6, 5, 3 rotis. Average (5) = representative leader. Big eaters' surplus fills small eaters' deficit.

The Mathematical Backbone

Average = Sum of all observations / n
Sum = Average × n (Formula 2 — used 80% of the time!)

2. The "Highway Method" (Assumed Average / Deviation)

Ultimate mental shortcut — no bulky summations!

1. Pick Assumed Average (A) — round number ending in 0 or 5.
2. Calculate Deviations: (Value − A) for each item.
3. Find Net Effect: Add all deviations algebraically.
4. Actual Average = A + (Net Effect / n)
Average of 28, 25, 30, 35, 26. (Assume A = 30)
Deviations: −2, −5, 0, +5, −4. Net = −6.
Actual Avg = 30 + (−6/5) = 30 − 1.2 = 28.8
Average of 112, 108, 115, 120, 110, 105. (Assume A = 110)
Deviations: +2, −2, +5, +10, 0, −5. Net = +10.
Actual Avg = 110 + (10/6) = 111.67

Avg age of 30 students = 12 yrs. Teacher included → avg becomes 13. Teacher's age?
Sum students = 30×12 = 360. Sum with teacher = 31×13 = 403.
Teacher's age = 403 − 360 = 43 years

4. Special Series & Arithmetic Progressions

If numbers have a constant gap, average is anchored to the center. Don't use sum formula!

Odd terms → Average = Exact Middle Term
Even terms → Average = Average of Two Middle Terms
SeriesSum FormulaAverage Formula
Natural (1,2,3...)n(n+1)/2(n+1)/2
Even (2,4,6...)n(n+1)n + 1
Odd (1,3,5...)n²n
Squares (1²,2²...)n(n+1)(2n+1)/6(n+1)(2n+1)/6
Cubes (1³,2³...)[n(n+1)]²/4n(n+1)²/4
Viral Shortcut (10 Consecutive Numbers): Sum of 10 consecutive numbers (e.g. 31 to 40) → find 5th term (35) and append '5' → Sum = 355
Average of first 15 natural numbers = (15+1)/2 = 8
Average of squares of first 10 natural numbers = (10+1)(2×10+1)/6 = 11×21/6 = 38.5

5. The "Chair Method" for Consecutive Numbers

  • Odd Chairs: Average sits in the physical center chair.
  • Even Chairs: Average sits "on the floor" between the two middle chairs.
  • Gap Rules: Natural gap = 1; Even/Odd gap = 2.

6. Weighted Averages & Group Ratios

Combined Avg = (n₁A₁ + n₂A₂) / (n₁ + n₂)
Never multiply large numbers — simplify group sizes into a ratio first!
20 Boys (Avg 15) + 30 Girls (Avg 16). Ratio = 2:3.
Combined Avg = (2×15 + 3×16) / 5 = 78/5 = 15.6
3 groups: A(25, avg 80), B(35, avg 70), C(40, avg 60).
Combined Avg = (25×80 + 35×70 + 40×60) / 100 = 6850/100 = 68.5
Alligation Shortcut: Need ratio of two groups? →
n₁/n₂ = |Combined Avg − A₂| / |A₁ − Combined Avg|

7. Group Splitting (Overlapping vs Non-Overlapping)

Overlapping (Parts > Total n): Sum of Parts − Total Sum = Overlapping Element
Non-Overlapping (Parts < Total n): Total Sum − Sum of Parts = Missing Element
Avg of 11 results = 50. First 6 avg = 49, last 6 avg = 52. Find 6th result.
Total = 550. Sum first 6 = 294. Sum last 6 = 312. Sum parts = 606 (6th counted twice).
6th result = 606 − 550 = 56

8. Entry, Exit & Replacement (High Frequency)

Focus on the Net Impact of Change. Use Burden/Contributor philosophy.

EventAvg IncreasesAvg Decreases
Entry (Joining)Newcomer = Contributor (> old avg)Newcomer = Burden (< old avg)
Exit (Leaving)Exiter was a BurdenExiter was a Contributor
Replacement: Difference = n × Change in Avg
Direct Entry: New Entrant = New Avg + (Old Count × Increase)
Cricketer: Score needed = New Avg + (Old Innings × Increase)
10 people (Avg 14). One joins, New Avg 15.
New person = 15 + (10 × 1) = 25
25 boys, avg 40 kg. One boy leaves, avg becomes 39. Weight of boy who left?
Old total = 1000. New total = 24×39 = 936.
Boy who left = 1000 − 936 = 64 kg (Contributor — avg dropped!)
8 people, avg increases by 2.5 kg when new person replaces one weighing 65 kg.
Difference = 8 × 2.5 = 20.
New person = 65 + 20 = 85 kg

9. Advanced Applications

A. Average Speed (Crucial Trap!)

NEVER take direct arithmetic mean of speeds!
Avg Speed = 2v₁v₂ / (v₁ + v₂) (for equal distances)
Car A→B at 40 km/h, returns at 60 km/h.
Avg Speed = 2×40×60 / (40+60) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h
(NOT 50!)

B. Mistake Correction (Data Entry Errors)

New Avg = Old Avg + (Correct Value − Wrong Value) / n
10 numbers, avg = 15. Number misread as 26 instead of 62.
Correct Avg = 15 + (62−26)/10 = 15 + 3.6 = 18.6

C. Mixture & Alligation

Averages are the mathematical heart of Alligation. The "distance" from individual averages to combined average gives the mixing ratio.

D. Prime Number Trap

Prime numbers have NO constant gap — you CANNOT use AP shortcuts! Must sum directly.
Average of first 5 primes (2,3,5,7,11) = 28/5 = 5.6 (no shortcut!)

10. Common Traps & Self-Check

• Rates (speed, price) over unequal bases → harmonic-style formula, NOT simple average!
• "Average increases when included" = secretly an Entry problem.
• Overlapping/Missing: check if parts add up to more or less than n.
Sanity Check: Average MUST lie between min and max values!
