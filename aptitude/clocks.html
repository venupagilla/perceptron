Clocks

Complete Notes on Clock Angles, Speeds & Faulty Clocks

1. Fundamentals: Clock Hands & Speeds

A standard analog clock = 360° circle, 12 hour spaces, 60 minute spaces.

HandCoversSpeed

Spatial Note: 1 minute space = 6°. 5 minute spaces = 30° = 1 hour space.

2. Finding the Angle Between Hands

θ = |(11m/2) − 30h| = |5.5m − 30h|
Angle at 4:20?
θ = |5.5 × 20 − 30 × 4| = |110 − 120| = 10°
A clock always forms TWO angles (interior + reflex) adding to 360°. If θ > 180°, subtract from 360° for the smaller angle.

4. Faulty Clocks

  • Perfect clock: hands coincide every 65 5/11 minutes.
  • Fast clock: hands coincide in < 65 5/11 min (gaining).
  • Slow clock: hands coincide in > 65 5/11 min (losing).
Clock gains 5 min/hour. Set right at 8:00 AM. At 1:00 PM actual?
Elapsed = 5 hrs. Total gain = 5 × 5 = 25 min.
Shows 1:25 PM

5. Visual Reflections: Mirror & Water Images

Mirror Image (Horizontal Reflection)

Subtract time from 11:60 (AM) or 23:60 (PM)
Mirror image of 8:20 = 11:60 − 8:20 = 3:40

Water Image (Vertical Reflection)

Subtract from 18:30 (if mins ≤ 30) or 17:90 (if mins > 30)
Water image of 4:40 (mins > 30): 17:90 − 4:40 = 13:50 = 1:50

6. Striking Clocks

Number of Intervals = Number of Strikes − 1
Time depends on INTERVALS, not strikes!
Clock takes 12 sec to strike 4 times. Time for 8 strikes?
4 strikes = 3 intervals → 1 interval = 4s
8 strikes = 7 intervals → 7 × 4 = 28 seconds

7. Advanced: Equation of Time Trick

When will hands form angle θ between H and (H+1)?
m = (2/11) × (30H ± θ)
Use '+' for first time after H | Use '−' for second time before (H+1)
Between 3 and 4, hands overlap (0°)?
m = (2/11) × (30 × 3 − 0) = 180/11 = 16 4/11 minutes past 3

8. Degree of Overlap in Faulty Clocks

Clock gains 5°/min. Real time = 1:00 PM. Normal angle = 30°.
Gain in 60 min = 60 × 5° = 300°.
False angle = |30 − 300| = 270° (or smaller: 90°)

9. Calendar Integration

  • To lose a day: clock must lose 24 hrs total. If loses 5 min/hr → 288 hrs (12 days).
  • To gain a day: same logic, 288 hrs to show correct time on different date.

10. Two Clocks Racing

Must differ by 12 hours (720 min) to show same time again.
Clock A gains 2 min/hr. Clock B loses 1 min/hr.
Relative diff = 3 min/hr.
Time = 720/3 = 240 hours
